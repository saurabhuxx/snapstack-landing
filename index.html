Capture

Capture any part of a webpage

Open the extension and pick how you want to capture the page: a full page screenshot, the visible area, or a selected area.

Full page

Capture an entire webpage from top to bottom — a full page screen capture without stitching it yourself.

Visible area

Grab exactly what is currently visible on screen. The Screenshot action in SnapStack is built for that.

Selected area

Drag to select the exact part of the webpage you want. Useful when you only need one block, not the whole site.

Projects

Stop searching. Start organizing.

Create a project and keep related screenshots together. A project is a piece of work, not a website — shots from several sites can share one folder, while each site still groups into its own session.

Local-first

Your screenshots. Your computer.

SnapStack is built for local screenshot management. Captures, projects, and folders stay on your device. There is no account to create and no cloud library to log into.

The extension reminds you of that in the product itself: your screenshots stay on your device.

Questions

A few straight answers

What is SnapStack?

SnapStack is a screenshot organizer and Chrome screenshot tool. It captures webpage screenshots and keeps them on your computer, grouped by project and by site session.

Can I capture a full webpage in Chrome?

Yes. Use Full page for a complete scroll, Screenshot for the visible area, or Select area when you only want part of the page.

Does SnapStack upload my screenshots?

SnapStack is local-first. Screenshots and project folders stay on your device. You do not need an account to use the extension.

How do I export screenshots?

Save captures individually or together, and export a session as a ZIP when you want a single archive.
