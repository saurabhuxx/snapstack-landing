Questions? Answers.

A few straight answers

What is SnapStack?

SnapStack is a Chrome screenshot extension that helps you capture, organize, and manage screenshots locally. You can capture full webpages, visible areas, or specific parts of a page.

Can I take a full-page screenshot in Chrome?

Yes. SnapStack lets you capture an entire webpage from top to bottom, making it easy to save full-page screenshots without manually stitching images together.

Can I capture only a specific area of a webpage?

Yes. You can select a specific area of the webpage and capture only what you need.

Where are my SnapStack screenshots saved?

SnapStack is designed for local screenshot management. You can choose how and where your screenshots are saved based on the available save options.

Can I organize screenshots into projects?

Yes. You can create projects and keep screenshots related to a specific project together instead of having them scattered across your Downloads folder.

What are screenshot sessions?

Sessions let you group screenshots taken during a particular task or browsing session, making it easier to keep related screenshots together.

Can I save multiple screenshots at once?

Yes. SnapStack supports bulk actions so you can manage and save multiple screenshots together instead of handling each file individually.

Can I export my screenshots?

Yes. You can export your screenshots together as a ZIP file when you need to move or share a collection of screenshots.

Does SnapStack upload my screenshots to the cloud?

No. SnapStack is built around local screenshot management. Your screenshots are handled locally rather than being uploaded to a SnapStack cloud service.

Is SnapStack free to use?

SnapStack is available as a Chrome extension. You can install it from the Chrome Web Store and start using it to capture and organize your screenshots.
